Outbreak Survival Manual

A tiny field book of useful skills, ordered by how much they matter when everything gets stupid. Start with critical basics, then unlock the fancy goblin competence later.

Chapters

Ordered by importance first, difficulty second.

01

Water Safety

Critical Easy

Water is priority zero. Mystery puddle soup is not a beverage, no matter how cinematic the lighting is.

  • Filter visible dirt before boiling.
  • Boil when possible.
  • Store clean water away from dirty containers.
02

First Aid Basics

Critical Medium

Most disasters are less dramatic than movies and more annoying: cuts, sprains, infection, bad decisions.

  • Stop bleeding with pressure.
  • Clean wounds before wrapping.
  • Keep bandages dry and accessible.
03

Shelter & Warmth

High Medium

Shelter is not just a roof. It is wind control, dryness, warmth, and not sleeping like a damp sandwich.

  • Block wind first.
  • Keep yourself off cold ground.
  • Stay dry before trying to stay warm.
04

Scavenging Rules

High Medium

Good scavenging is boring. You enter, take useful items, leave. You do not start decorating your backpack with junk loot.

  • Know what you need before entering.
  • Prioritize water, food, medicine, batteries.
  • Leave before the building starts feeling like a plot device.
05

Radio Discipline

Medium Easy

A radio is not a podcast studio. Clear messages save battery, time, and everyone’s remaining patience.

  • Say who you are.
  • Say where you are.
  • Say what you need.
06

Quiet Movement

Medium Medium

Noise discipline is just stealth with fewer ninja fantasies. If it rattles, tape it. If it squeaks, fix it.

  • Secure loose gear.
  • Step slowly before stepping fast.
  • Avoid glass, metal scraps, and dramatic entrances.
